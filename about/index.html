About

Essays on AI, written for curious readers and practitioners alike.

The name comes from “Attention Is All You Need” (Vaswani et al., 2017), the paper that introduced the Transformer. Almost every major AI system since then descends from it.

The essays here try to work on two levels. The main text should make sense without a machine-learning background. Where there is math, code or a paper worth digging into, it sits in optional detours for readers who want the details.
